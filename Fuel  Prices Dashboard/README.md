# ⛽ Global Fuel Prices Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes global fuel prices across different countries to identify pricing trends, taxation impacts, subsidy effects, and relationships between crude oil and retail fuel prices. The dashboard was developed to provide insights into fuel price variations and factors influencing fuel costs worldwide.

---

## Business Problem

Fuel prices vary significantly across countries due to factors such as taxes, subsidies, income levels, and crude oil prices. Understanding these factors can help policymakers, businesses, and consumers make informed decisions.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze global fuel price trends.
- Compare petrol, diesel, and LPG prices across countries.
- Examine the impact of taxation on fuel prices.
- Evaluate the relationship between crude oil prices and petrol prices.
- Identify countries with the highest and lowest fuel prices.

---

## Key KPIs

- Average Petrol Price
- Average Diesel Price
- Average LPG Price

---

## Dashboard Features

### Fuel Price Comparison
Compares petrol, diesel, and LPG prices across countries.

### Tax Impact Analysis
Examines how fuel taxation affects retail fuel prices.

### Income Level Analysis
Analyzes fuel prices across different income groups.

### Crude Oil vs Petrol Prices
Explores the relationship between global crude oil prices and petrol prices.

### Top 10 Highest Fuel Price Countries
Identifies countries with the highest average fuel prices.

### Fuel Price Trends
Tracks fuel price changes over time.

---

## Key Insights

- Countries with higher fuel taxes generally experience higher retail fuel prices.
- Significant differences exist between fuel prices across regions and income groups.
- Petrol prices tend to follow movements in crude oil prices.
- Some countries maintain lower prices through fuel subsidies.
