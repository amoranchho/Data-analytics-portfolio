# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes sales performance by tracking revenue, profit, costs, customer contributions, and product performance. The objective is to provide stakeholders with actionable insights to improve profitability and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Businesses need to understand which products, customers, and periods generate the highest revenue and profit. This dashboard helps identify key drivers of business performance and highlights opportunities for growth.

---

## Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance.
* Track revenue, profit, and profit margin.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Analyze customer contributions to revenue.
* Examine monthly sales trends.
* Support business decision-making through data visualization.

---

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Data Modeling

---

## Key KPIs

* Total Revenue
* Total Cost
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin %
* Total Units Sold

---

## Dashboard Features

### Revenue Trend Analysis

Tracks revenue performance across months to identify seasonal patterns.

### Product Performance

Compares revenue and profit generated by each product category.

### Customer Analysis

Highlights customers contributing the most revenue.

### Profit Margin Analysis

Ranks products based on profitability.

### KPI Monitoring

Provides a high-level overview of business performance.

---

## Key Insights

* Chocolate Chip generated the highest revenue.
* Snickerdoodle achieved the highest profit margin.
* October recorded the strongest revenue performance.
* ACME Bites was the highest-revenue customer.
* The business maintained a profit margin of approximately 58%.
